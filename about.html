Smart money moves made simple — free Canadian financial tools built with transparency and privacy in mind.

What is FiggyBank?

FiggyBank is a free Canadian financial calculator suite built by Rick Minji — a financial enthusiast committed to making money management accessible to all Canadians.

We provide 20+ financial calculators covering mortgages, taxes, retirement planning, business valuation, and more — all 100% free, with no sign-ups, no paywalls, and no hidden fees.

Our Principles

🔒 Privacy-First: All calculations run client-side in your browser. We don't store, sell, or share your financial data. Ever.

🆓 100% Free: No freemium tricks, no premium tiers, no paywalls. If it's on FiggyBank, it's free.

🇨🇦 Canadian-Focused: Tax brackets, CPP, OAS, TFSA, RRSP, FHSA — everything is built for the Canadian financial landscape.

📖 Educational: We don't just give you numbers — our blog explains the "why" behind every calculation, helping you make informed decisions.

Who Built This?

FiggyBank was created by Rick Minji, a Canadian software developer and personal finance enthusiast. After years of struggling to find simple, trustworthy financial tools tailored to Canada, Rick decided to build them himself.

What started as a weekend project has grown into a comprehensive suite of calculators and educational content used by thousands of Canadians every month.
