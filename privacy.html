Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1. Introduction

At FiggyBank, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

Personal Information

We do not collect or store any personal financial data. All calculator inputs (salaries, mortgage amounts, investment figures, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser and never sent to our servers.

Analytics & Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may collect non-personal information:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Tracks anonymous usage data (page views, session duration, device type) to help us improve the site. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Google AdSense: Displays advertisements and uses cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings.

3. Contact Form

When you use our contact form, your message is sent via FormSubmit.co to our email address (rajankchopra@gmail.com). We do not store contact form submissions on our servers.

4. Affiliate Links

Some blog posts and pages may contain Amazon affiliate links. If you purchase through these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are valuable.

5. No User Accounts

FiggyBank does not require user accounts, logins, or passwords. You remain completely anonymous.

6. Client-Side Calculations

All financial calculations (tax, mortgage, investment, etc.) are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your input data never leaves your device.

7. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Theme preference: Stores your light/dark mode choice in localStorage.
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous tracking cookies (you can disable these in your browser).
  • Google AdSense: Advertising cookies (you can opt out via Google Ads Settings).

8. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: How Google uses data
  • FormSubmit.co: Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Associates: Privacy Notice

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Disable cookies in your browser settings
  • Opt out of Google Analytics via Google Analytics Opt-out
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
